The problem

Tried a voice clone and got robotic mispronunciations or flat delivery? The failures cluster in three places, and only one of them is the engine:

Bad input

Room echo, mouth clicks, inconsistent mic distance, takes that are too short. Engines reproduce exactly what you give them, flaws included.

Pronunciation surprises

Most engines mispronounce names and domain terms out of the box. Normal and fixable — but not if you don't plan for it.

Judging with your eyes

"98% match" scores are comforting and mostly useless. One engine with strong scores still failed the only test that mattered: the person didn't recognize themselves.

How it works

  1. 1. Record

    Follow the 60-minute plan: topics that make you talk like you, a calibration question every session, and takes that survive the process.

  2. 2. QA

    Run the four checks on every take — size, duration, silence scan, corruption — with free tools like ffmpeg, before any engine sees them.

  3. 3. Judge blind

    Same script on two or more engines, defaults, badges stripped, randomized labels. Your ear is the only judge — machine scores don't vote.
